2. Exclusions & What Is Not Covered

The BYND+ warranty is intended to ensure product integrity under intended use. The warranty does not cover:

  • Damage resulting from accidental dropping, water exposure, immersion, or extreme environmental moisture.
  • Electrical damage caused by uncertified third-party power adapters or severe electrical line surges.
  • Unauthorized modification, disassembly, or repair attempted by third-party technicians.
  • Cosmetic scratches, scuffs, or minor aesthetic wear that do not impair therapeutic irradiance.
  • Dietary supplements and consumable items (which are covered under our separate Returns & Refunds Policy).
