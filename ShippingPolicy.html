The moment your package is scanned by our carrier partner, you will receive an automated shipping confirmation email containing a direct carrier tracking link. You can follow your delivery progress in real time through:

Claims & Support

5. Transit Damage or Missing Packages

Every shipment is fully insured by BYND Human against loss or damage in transit. In the rare event that your shipment arrives with outer box damage or internal hardware issues:

  1. Please inspect outer packaging upon delivery and take clear photographs of any noticeable crushing, puncture, or tears.
  2. Contact our advisory team within 48 hours of delivery through our Contact form or email support@byndhuman.com with your order number.
  3. Our team will file an immediate carrier insurance claim and coordinate an expedited replacement unit at zero cost to you.
