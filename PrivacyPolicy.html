Data Protection & Integrity

BYND Human Privacy Policy

We value your trust and are committed to safeguarding your personal data. Learn how we collect, protect, utilize, and respect your information across our website and services.

Policy & Compliance Notice
Standard e-commerce, transaction protection, and customer advisory data practices are detailed below. Specific regional data regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and state consumer privacy frameworks) are marked for final review and approval by BYND Human legal counsel.
Data Collection

1. Information We Collect

When you browse our digital storefront, purchase a BYND+ device, subscribe to our supplement formulations, or consult with our advisory team, we collect only the necessary information required to serve your routine:

Account & Contact Details
Name, email address, physical shipping address, billing address, and phone number for delivery updates.
Orders & Subscriptions
Purchased hardware devices, supplement recurring intervals (30/60/90 days), order timestamps, and transaction history.
Support & Advisory
Messages submitted through our contact form, warranty registrations, and protocol inquiries.
Purpose & Application

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal data is handled strictly to provide, improve, and support the BYND Human experience. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Fulfill Orders: Process transactions, dispatch hardware safely, manage tracked shipments, and deliver scheduled subscription orders.
  • Provide Customer Support: Answer device operation questions, coordinate warranty service, and assist with returns or exchanges.
  • Secure Transactions: Prevent fraudulent orders, verify customer identity for high-value hardware shipments, and uphold payment security.
  • Service Communications: Send order confirmations, carrier tracking links, subscription renewal reminders, and critical device safety notices.
  • Optional Updates: Share new releases, protocol guides, and wellness articles — only if you opt in. You may unsubscribe at any time with a single click.
Browsing Technologies

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and lightweight local storage to deliver a fluid browsing experience:

•
Essential Cookies: Retain shopping cart contents, checkout session states, and currency selections. These are necessary for the website to function.
•
Analytics & Performance: Anonymous data that helps us understand how visitors interact with our pages, identify slow load times, and improve overall design responsiveness.
•
Cookie Management: You can choose to disable non-essential cookies via your web browser settings at any time without losing basic store navigation.
Third Parties

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We only share minimal data with trusted service providers necessary to operate our business:

  • Payment Gateways: Payments are processed via PCI-DSS Level 1 certified processors (Stripe, Shopify Payments, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay). Your raw credit card details are tokenized and encrypted; BYND Human never stores full card numbers.
  • Fulfillment & Couriers: Shipping carriers receive your name, destination address, and phone number solely for physical delivery and tracking purposes.
  • Cloud & Security Providers: Trusted enterprise infrastructure providers who host our platform and protect against cybersecurity threats.
Protection

5. Data Security & Retention

We employ robust administrative, technical, and physical measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse:

256-Bit SSL/TLS Encryption
All communications and checkout data transmitted between your browser and our servers are encrypted.
Proportionate Retention
We retain customer order records only as long as necessary to honor warranties, fulfill subscriptions, and comply with tax laws.
Your Controls

6. Your Privacy Rights & Inquiries

Depending on your location, you have the right to request access to the personal data we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your records. You may also update your subscription settings or opt out of promotional communications at any time.

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact our privacy team at privacy@byndhuman.com or reach out through our Contact page.
